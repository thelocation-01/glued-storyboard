# Publishing responsibly

Glued helps assemble videos; it does not clear rights or guarantee monetization.

Before publishing:

1. Confirm you have commercial rights to every image, music track, sound, font, logo, and quoted clip.
2. Keep a source and rights register for factual claims and media.
3. Add meaningful original narration, analysis, structure, and editing. Avoid mass-produced, near-identical slideshow videos.
4. Use the platform's synthetic/altered-content disclosure when realistic AI-generated scenes, voices, people, events, or places require it.
5. Review captions, claims, thumbnail, title, and description manually.
6. Keep project files and evidence of your original work.

Policies change. Check the current rules of YouTube or any other platform at the time of upload.
